A bit of wedding-newspaper fun

The Singles' Column

At our reception you'll find a one-of-a-kind wedding newspaper, full of stories, games, and little surprises — and a personals page for our wonderful single friends.

How it works

Place a personal ad

One of our favorite features is "The Personals" — playful little advertisements for the single guests at our wedding, so a new spark might just fly at the reception. If you're single and feeling brave, submit a photo and a short bio below, and we'll print it in the paper. Because the newspaper goes to print well before the big day, all submissions must be in by June 30, 2026.

The fine print

A few simple guidelines

To keep everything looking sharp in print, please follow these when you submit.

01

The Photo

One clear, friendly photo of just you. A JPG or PNG file, up to 10 MB in size.

02

Orientation

Portrait or square works best — it will be printed in a narrow newspaper column, so avoid wide landscape shots.

03

The Bio

A short, punchy paragraph — who you are, what you're looking for, your best quality. Around 280 characters.

04

Keep it classy

Fun, kind, and tasteful, please. We may lightly edit entries so everything fits neatly on the page.

05

Deadline

Submissions close June 30, 2026 — we need time to lay out and print the newspaper before the wedding.
