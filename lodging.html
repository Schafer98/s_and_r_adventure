For our traveling guests

Stay & Explore

Towering Oaks sits in the peaceful countryside about 45 minutes north of Dallas. For those making the trip, here are some comfortable places to stay and a few favorite things to do.

Where to Stay

A comfortable home base

Denton is the liveliest town near the venue — about a 25–30 minute drive — with plenty to do. If you'd rather be closer to Towering Oaks, Gainesville is roughly 15 minutes away. We'd suggest booking early.

In Denton — our recommendation

Hilton

Embassy Suites Denton

Convention Center · ~25 min to venue

A polished all-suite hotel with a free cooked-to-order breakfast and a nightly reception. Spacious two-room suites — lovely for families.

Things to Do

Make a little trip of it

The venue is wonderfully tucked away, so we've gathered a few ways to fill a free afternoon — from right around the corner all the way to downtown Dallas.

Near the venue

The Outdoors

Ray Roberts Lake State Park

Hiking and biking trails, swimming beaches, fishing and boating on a beautiful lake — a lovely way to spend a morning.

Map & Info

Entertainment

WinStar World Casino

One of the world's largest casinos — live concerts, golf, and dozens of restaurants — about 20 minutes north in Thackerville.

Map & Info

Small-town Charm

Historic Gainesville

A walkable downtown of antique shops and cafés, the Morton Museum, and the Frank Buck Zoo, home to 130-plus animals.
